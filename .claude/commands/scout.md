# /scout — find my next Short

Follow these steps in order.

1. **Get ready.** Find today's date. Read `CLAUDE.md` (who I am, who my viewers are, what a good idea looks like) and `made-shorts.md` (what I've already made).

2. **Send out three helpers at the same time.** In a single message, start all three of these helpers together, not one after another. Tell each one today's date.
   - `claude-news` — what's new with Claude this week
   - `other-labs-news` — what's new from the other AI labs this week
   - `beginner-questions` — what people are asking about AI this week

3. **Run the checker.** When all three are back, give every idea they found to the `checker` helper, along with today's date. It throws out anything older than 7 days, anything without a date, and anything I've already made.

4. **Pick the best three** from what the checker kept. Prefer ideas that:
   - a complete beginner would understand and want to watch,
   - have a clear, real reason to post this week,
   - have something to show on screen.
   Try to mix sources (not three Claude stories if the others are good). If fewer than three survive, show only those and say so honestly. Never make up an idea or a source to fill the gap.

5. **Show me the three ideas** in chat, each in this format:
   - **Title:**
   - **Hook:**
   - **Why now:**
   - **Source:** link (publish date)
   Under them, add a short "Thrown out" list: each rejected idea and why, one line each.

6. **Save** the same content to `ideas/YYYY-MM-DD.md` using today's date.

7. **Ask me** which idea (if any) I'm going to make. Add each one I pick to the bottom of `made-shorts.md` as a new line, so it never comes up again.
