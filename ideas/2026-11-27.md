# Short ideas — 2026-11-27

## 1. Claude now shows you a monthly recap
- **Hook:** "Claude just made you a 'year in review'… except it's every month."
- **Why now:** The monthly recap in Settings left beta on Nov 24. It's on every personal plan including Free, on web, desktop and phone, and you can delete it. Anyone can open theirs today and film it.
- **Source:** https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes (Nov 24, 2026)

## 2. Google search can now look at your Drive and Wallet
- **Hook:** "You can now ask Google about your own receipts. Should you?"
- **Why now:** Google's AI Mode in Search can now use your own Drive and Wallet if you switch it on. Wallet access is read-only and can't move money. It's rolling out over the next few weeks.
- **Source:** https://9to5google.com/2026/11/23/ai-mode-wallet-gemini/ (Nov 23, 2026 — date from the link only; check before filming)

## 3. Claude can now send files straight to your apps
- **Hook:** "Stop downloading and re-uploading. Claude can just hand the file over."
- **Why now:** Since Nov 24, Claude can send files to apps you've connected to it and save files from them. Paid plans only and still rolling out.
- **Source:** https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes (Nov 24, 2026)
