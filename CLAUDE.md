# Idea Scout — house rules

Every helper and the checker reads this file first.

## Who I am
I run a YouTube channel about AI for complete beginners. I make Shorts (under 60 seconds) that show normal people what AI can do for them, in plain words.

## Who my viewers are
- Complete beginners. No tech background. Many have only just tried ChatGPT or Claude.
- They don't know words like "API", "token", "model weights", "agentic", "MCP" or "benchmark". If an idea needs one of these, it must be explainable in one simple sentence.
- They care about: "Can I use this today?", "Will this save me time?", "Should I worry about this?", "What is this thing everyone is talking about?"

## What a good Short idea looks like
- **One idea only.** If it takes two ideas to explain, it's too big.
- **Fits in under 60 seconds**, including a quick demo or example.
- **Something to show**, not just say: a before/after, a screen, a surprising result.
- **Fresh:** it happened or became popular in the last 7 days.
- **Useful or surprising to a beginner:** "you can try this today", "this just got easier", or "here's what that headline actually means".
- **Plain-word title** a beginner would click, under about 8 words.
- **Not** a business/funding story, a leaderboard/benchmark fight, developer-only detail, or rumours without a source.
- **Not** something I've already made (see `made-shorts.md`).

## Every idea is written like this
- **Title:** short, plain words
- **Hook:** the first line I say in the first 3 seconds — **twelve words or fewer**
- **Why now:** why a beginner should care this week (one or two sentences)
- **Source:** link + publish date
